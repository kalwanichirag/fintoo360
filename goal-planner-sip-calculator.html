How this goal planner works

The calculator works backwards from your goal. It first grows your current savings at the expected return, then estimates the starting monthly SIP required to fund the remaining amount. If you select a step-up, the SIP increases once every year.

Keep assumptions realistic. Market returns are not guaranteed. Review your goal annually and adjust the SIP when your income, timeline, or goal cost changes.

Common questions

Should I use a step-up SIP?+

A yearly increase can reduce the SIP you need today, but choose a rate you can sustain as your income grows.

Does this include inflation?+

Enter the future amount you expect to need. If your goal is in today's prices, inflate it before using it here.

Are returns guaranteed?+

No. The result is an estimate based on the return assumption you provide.
