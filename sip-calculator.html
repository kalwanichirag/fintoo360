What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) means investing a fixed amount into a mutual fund at regular intervals, usually monthly, instead of putting in one lump sum. It suits income you receive periodically — a salary, a freelance payout — and builds the habit of investing before you get a chance to spend it.

Because each instalment buys units at a different price, a SIP averages your purchase cost across market ups and downs — a benefit known as rupee-cost averaging — while still compounding the entire invested corpus over time.
