Can Indians invest abroad?

Yes. Here's how investing abroad from India works.

The limit

USD 250,000.

Under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, you can invest up to USD 250,000 a year in global markets.

The tax

Up to ₹10 lakh.

Invest up to ₹10 lakh overseas in a financial year without TCS. A 20% TCS applies only to the amount exceeding ₹10 lakh and can be claimed as a tax credit when filing your income tax return.

The process

In your name.

We guide you from your Indian bank to a global account held in your name.

Yes. Under LRS, resident Indians can invest up to USD 250,000 a year in US stocks, ETFs and bonds.
