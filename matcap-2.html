Can Indians invest abroad?

Yes. Here's how investing abroad from India works.

The limit:

USD 250,000.

Under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme, you can invest up to USD 250,000 a year in global markets.

The tax:

₹10 lakh.

No TCS up to ₹10 lakh a year. Above that, 20% TCS applies, which you can claim back in your tax return.

The process:

In your name.

We guide you from your Indian bank to a global account held in your name.

Note: LRS limit and TCS rates as of FY 2026-27. Rules can change; confirm with your bank or tax adviser before remitting.

FAQ

Questions about investing globally.

Can Indians invest in Nasdaq and US stocks?

Yes. Under LRS, resident Indians can invest up to USD 250,000 a year in US stocks, ETFs and bonds.

How do I invest abroad from India?

You remit money under LRS to a global account in your name. We guide you through every step.

How is MatCap different from US investing apps?

Apps let you buy stocks yourself. We build and manage a global portfolio around your goals.

Can you help with my ESOPs and RSUs?

Yes. We help you transfer them into one account and plan sales around vesting periods.
