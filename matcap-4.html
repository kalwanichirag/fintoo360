Why global diversification matters

Reduce single-country risk

When most of your wealth, income and property sit in one economy, one downturn can affect all of it. Spreading across regions reduces that dependence.

Reach businesses you cannot buy at home

Many of the world's largest companies and fastest-growing industries are listed overseas. A global portfolio gives you access to them.

Hold some wealth in other currencies

Children's education, travel and retirement abroad are often funded in foreign currency. Holding global assets can help match goals to currencies.

Balance what you already own

Concentrated holdings, such as employer stock, ESOPs or a family business, need a plan. Global diversification gives that plan somewhere to go.

All investments carry market risk, and diversification does not guarantee profit or protect against loss.

Clarity across borders

A compliant route to global investing

For resident Indians, offshore investing follows defined RBI and tax rules. We help you understand the steps, plan remittances and keep reporting in view.

1Remit within LRS

Resident individuals can remit up to USD 250,000 per financial year under the RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme.

2Plan for TCS

TCS applies at 20% on investment remittances above ₹10 lakh in a financial year, and can be adjusted against your income tax.

3Keep reporting current

Foreign assets and income must be disclosed in your Indian tax return. Remittances to jurisdictions flagged by the RBI are not permitted.

4Invest transparently

We work within permitted channels and do not advise on structures designed to avoid tax or hide assets.

Information as of October 2026. Rules change, so confirm your position with your bank and a tax professional.

Common questions

What is global diversification?

It means spreading your investments across countries, currencies, sectors and asset types instead of relying on one market. The aim is to reduce the impact of any single region's downturn on your overall wealth.

How much do I need to start a global portfolio?

There is no single rule. The right size depends on your goals, your LRS limit and the costs involved. In your free session, an adviser will tell you honestly whether a global portfolio makes sense at your level.

Are international mutual funds a good way to diversify?

They can be one option, but Indian mutual funds share an industry-wide overseas investment cap, and many international schemes have paused or limited fresh investments in 2026. Availability changes often, so an adviser can show you what is open and what the alternatives are.

Can I invest in offshore markets or tax havens?

Resident Indians can invest abroad through RBI-permitted routes such as LRS. Remittances to jurisdictions flagged by the RBI are not allowed, and all foreign assets and income must be reported in India. We help you invest globally within these rules and do not advise on tax avoidance or concealment.

Can my ESOPs and RSUs be part of the plan?

Yes. Vested ESOPs, RSUs and overseas holdings can be consolidated into your overall strategy, which helps reduce the risk of holding too much of one employer's stock.
