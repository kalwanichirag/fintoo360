Why we exist

Most financial advice in India is paid for by the product you end up buying.

So, quietly, it is shaped by the product too.

We built Fintoo the other way round.

No product pays us to recommend it. Your goals decide what we recommend.

Which means the only way we do well is when you do.

What we believe

Our values have been the same since day one: customer centricity, honest determination, and responsibility for your wealth. These are the beliefs they come from.

  1. 01

    Good intentions are not enough.

    Most advisers mean well. But incentives win in the long run, so we removed the incentive: we earn nothing from the products we recommend.

  2. 02

    A plan beats a portfolio of products.

    Investments, tax, insurance, loans, property and family are one system. We plan them together, because every decision moves the others.

  3. 03

    Doing nothing is sometimes the best advice.

    We are not paid per transaction, so we have no reason to keep you busy. Patience has made our clients more money than activity ever has.

  4. 04

    If it can't be explained simply, it shouldn't be sold.

    Every recommendation comes in plain language, with the risks as clear as the benefits. No jargon. No surprises in the fine print.

  5. 05

    Advice should be accountable.

    We write down what we recommend and why, so you can hold us to it at every review. Responsibility for your wealth is not a slogan; it is a record.

  6. 06

    Technology should serve judgement, not replace it.

    Our systems gather your finances and run the numbers. A person who knows your family makes the call, and stays with you when markets test your nerve.

Questions about Fintoo

What families usually want to know before they trust us with their finances.

What is Fintoo?

Fintoo is the brand of Fintoo Wealth Private Limited, a Mumbai-based SEBI-registered investment adviser (INA000020031). We offer fee-based financial planning and advice on investments, retirement, tax and insurance, combining in-house technology with experienced advisors and tax experts. We advise 500+ families and track over ₹5,000 crore of their assets.

Is Fintoo SEBI registered?

Yes. Fintoo Wealth Private Limited is registered with SEBI as a non-individual investment adviser, registration number INA000020031, valid from 26 March 2025 on a perpetual basis. It is also a member of BSE Administration and Supervision Limited (BASL) with ID 2252. You can verify the registration on SEBI's list of recognised intermediaries.

How does Fintoo make money?

Fintoo earns an advisory fee paid by its clients, agreed in writing before any work begins. We do not earn commission from fund houses, insurers or other product manufacturers on the products we recommend, so no product is worth more to us than another.

Is Fintoo's advice unbiased?

Our model is built to remove bias. As a SEBI-registered investment adviser we are paid by clients rather than product makers, SEBI's rules separate advice from distribution, and every recommendation is documented with its reasoning. We also disclose any conflict of interest, however small.

What services does Fintoo offer?

Financial planning is at the centre, supported by investment planning, retirement planning, tax planning, insurance planning, NRI taxation and help with tax notices. All advice is given in the context of one written plan for your family.

Who is Fintoo for?

Families whose finances have outgrown product-by-product buying: business owners, senior professionals, NRIs and returning Indians, families nearing retirement, and anyone who has received a large sum from an inheritance, property sale or business exit.

How much does Fintoo charge?

Your first consultation is free. After that, the fee depends on the scope of advice and is agreed in writing before we begin, within the limits SEBI sets for investment advisers. See our pricing page for current plans.

Does Fintoo hold my money?

No. Your investments stay in your own name, with the fund houses, depositories and banks where they are held. Fintoo advises you on what to do; you stay in control of your money.

Is my data safe with Fintoo?

Your data is encrypted before it travels over any network, stored on AWS cloud infrastructure, and protected by role-based access with OTPs. Our systems are regularly security-tested. We use your data only to advise you and do not sell or share it for marketing.

Where is Fintoo located?

Fintoo's registered office is at B/309, Dynasty Business Park, Andheri (East), Mumbai 400059. We advise clients across India and NRIs around the world, in person and online. You can reach us on +91 96998 00600 or online@fintoo.in.

How do I raise a complaint about Fintoo?

Write to our Compliance Officer, Nisha Harchekar, at nisha.harchekar@fintoo.in, or use the grievance mechanism on our website. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can escalate to SEBI through SCORES or use the Online Dispute Resolution (ODR) portal.

What is the difference between Fintoo and a mutual fund distributor or bank?

A distributor or bank relationship manager is usually paid commission by the products they sell, often through higher-cost regular plans. Fintoo is a SEBI-registered investment adviser paid a fee by you, recommending low-cost direct plans and advising on your whole financial picture rather than individual products.
