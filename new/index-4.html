Common questions.

What does Fintoo do?+

Fintoo is a SEBI-registered investment adviser and wealth platform. It brings financial planning, investing, tax and insurance advice together, and its app shows your net worth, goals, retirement readiness, mutual fund health and family finances in one view.

Is Fintoo registered with SEBI?+

Yes. Fintoo Wealth Private Limited is a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser (registration no. INA000020031) and a BASL member (membership no. 2252).

Can NRIs use Fintoo?+

Yes. Fintoo handles NRI tax filing, DTAA advisory, repatriation of funds including 15CA/15CB certificates, lower or NIL TDS certificates, and NRI property transactions, alongside investing in Indian and international markets.

What does zero commission on direct mutual funds mean?+

Direct plans carry no distributor commission. Through Fintoo you can invest in 2,500+ direct mutual funds across sectors at 0% commission.

How does Fintoo protect my data?+

Data is encrypted before it travels over any network, hosted on AWS cloud infrastructure, protected by role-based access with OTPs and captcha, and Fintoo's systems are regularly monitored and security tested.
