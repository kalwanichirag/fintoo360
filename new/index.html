The difference

Advice that isn't selling you something.

How a Fintoo relationship compares with platforms that earn from the products they distribute.

  1. Where advice starts

    With Fintoo: Your whole financial picture: income, assets, liabilities, goals and family.

    VS

    Product-led platforms: The product on the shelf this quarter.

  2. How your advisor is paid

    With Fintoo: By you, for advice. No commissions from the products we recommend.

    VS

    Product-led platforms: Commissions from what you buy, so the incentive is to sell.

  3. How often you hear from them

    With Fintoo: Scheduled reviews and a live view in Fintoo AI between them.

    VS

    Product-led platforms: When there is something new to sell.

  4. Who they answer to

    With Fintoo: A fiduciary duty under SEBI's Investment Adviser Regulations.

    VS

    Product-led platforms: Sales targets and distribution tie-ups.

FAQ

Questions people ask us

What is Fintoo?

Fintoo is a SEBI-registered investment adviser (INA000020031) that helps individuals and families plan, manage and grow their complete financial life, through financial planning, investment advice, retirement, tax, insurance, NRI and estate planning, supported by the Fintoo AI app.

Who is Fintoo for?

Professionals, business owners, entrepreneurs, high-net-worth families, pre-retirees, retirees and NRIs whose finances involve many connected decisions and benefit from one coordinated plan.

How is Fintoo different from an investment app?

An investment app helps you buy products. Fintoo starts with your whole financial picture, including goals, taxes, liabilities, protection and family, and advises on each decision within that plan.

What is Fintoo AI?

Fintoo AI is Fintoo's app. It brings your assets, liabilities, goals, investments and financial health into one view, highlights what needs attention and works alongside your Fintoo advisor.

Does Fintoo advise NRIs?

Yes. Fintoo helps NRIs with residential status, taxation, repatriation and investing in India from abroad, as part of one financial plan.

Is Fintoo registered with SEBI?

Yes. Fintoo is registered with SEBI as an investment adviser, registration number INA000020031.
