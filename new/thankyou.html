What should I prepare before the consultation?

Have a rough idea of your income, monthly expenses, existing investments, loans and insurance, and the goals you want to plan for. Statements help but are not required; your advisor will guide you through the rest.

Is the consultation really free? Are there any obligations?

Yes, the consultation is free, and there is no obligation to sign up for anything afterwards.

How do I join the video call for the meeting?

Use the secure meeting link in your confirmation email or calendar invite. Join a couple of minutes early from a laptop or phone with a stable internet connection.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes. Use the reschedule or cancel link in your confirmation email, or reach us on +91-9699 800 600 or online@fintoo.in.

How is my financial data kept private and secure?

Your data is encrypted before it travels over any network, access is role-based and protected with OTPs, it is hosted on Amazon Web Services, and our systems go through regular security assessments and penetration testing.
