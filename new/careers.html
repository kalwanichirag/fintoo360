Our culture

Six things we believe in.

These aren’t posters on a wall. They’re how we make decisions, give feedback and choose who joins us.

  • 01

    Client first, always

    We give advice the way we would want it for our own family. Every product, screen and conversation starts with what is right for the client.

  • 02

    Own it end to end

    You get real problems, not tickets. Take a problem from idea to launch, learn from the result and make it better.

  • 03

    Learn every day

    Markets, tax rules and technology change constantly. Curiosity is part of the job description here.

  • 04

    Say it plainly

    No jargon with clients, no politics with each other. Clear, honest feedback in both directions.

  • 05

    Build with care

    We handle people’s money and data. Accuracy, security and compliance are things we design for from day one.

  • 06

    Win together

    Planners, tax experts, engineers and marketers sit at the same table. Wins are shared, and so is the work.

FAQs

Questions candidates ask us

Something else on your mind? Write to support@fintoo.in.

Does Fintoo hire freshers?

Yes. We hire people at every stage of their career, including freshers and interns, across advisory, tax, engineering, design, marketing and operations. Choose “Fresher” in the experience field when you apply.

Do I need a finance background to work at Fintoo?

Not for every role. Advisory and tax roles need relevant qualifications, but engineering, design, marketing and operations roles value strong skills in your own field. You’ll learn the finance side with us.

What should my resume include, and which formats can I upload?

Include your recent roles, key projects or results, education and any certifications. You can upload a PDF, DOC or DOCX file up to 5 MB.

How long does the hiring process take?

It depends on the role and the number of rounds. We keep you informed at every step, and we let every applicant we speak to know the outcome.

Can I apply to more than one team?

Pick the team that fits you best. If you’d also be happy in another team, mention it in the “Why Fintoo” box and we will consider you for both.

Does Fintoo ever charge a fee for a job or interview?

No. Fintoo never asks candidates for money at any stage of hiring. If anyone asks you to pay in Fintoo’s name, do not pay, and report it to support@fintoo.in.
