Pricing FAQs

Straight answers on fees, plans and what is included.

Last reviewed 1 October 2026 by Mihir Shah, AVP, Financial Planning, Fintoo.

How much does Fintoo's financial planning cost?

Fintoo's Assisted Advisory plan is priced individually. Your fee depends on how many goals you have, the size and mix of your investments, the family members covered and how complex your tax and estate needs are. You get a quote after a free 30-minute call, and the fee is agreed in writing before any work starts.

Why is financial planning priced as custom?

A single professional with two goals needs far less work than a family with a business, property, ESOPs and assets abroad. Custom pricing means you pay for the plan you actually need, rather than a one-size package that is too thin for some families and too costly for others.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first 30-minute call with a Fintoo advisor is free and comes with no obligation. We use it to understand your goals and current investments, explain how we would approach your plan and quote your fee.

Does Fintoo earn commissions on the products it recommends?

No product pays Fintoo to recommend it. As a SEBI-registered investment adviser, Fintoo is paid by its clients for advice, and is required to keep advice separate from the sale of financial products. This means our recommendations depend only on your goals.

What is included in the ₹4,900 tax planning plan?

The tax planning plan, starting at ₹4,900, includes comprehensive tax planning, tax-loss harvesting, filing your income tax return, a dedicated tax manager and chat access to a tax expert. The final price depends on your income sources, such as capital gains, business income or foreign assets.

What if I have received an income tax notice?

Choose the Notices and Compliance plan. Your first consultation with a chartered accountant on the notice is complimentary. The CA team can then draft and file your response, attend hearings, and handle refunds, demands and rectifications, with a fee quoted for your case.

What does the Assisted Advisory plan include?

It includes a dedicated wealth manager, a comprehensive financial plan, will and estate planning, risk management, monthly reviews, private wealth management across direct mutual funds, direct equity, PMS, AIF, international equity, unlisted equity, bonds, debt and alternatives, portfolio rebalancing with execution, tax filing for you and your spouse, and chat with an expert.

How often will my financial plan be reviewed?

Assisted Advisory clients get monthly reviews. Your wealth manager tracks progress against each goal, suggests rebalancing when your portfolio drifts, and updates the plan when your income, family or goals change.

Can NRIs use Fintoo's services?

Yes. NRIs can use Fintoo for financial planning and for tax help, including NRI taxation, tax on Indian income, DTAA relief and income tax notices. Mention your residency on the first call so we can include it in your plan and quote.

How do I get started?

For financial planning, book a free 30-minute call and we will quote your fee. For tax planning, select an expert, pay for the consultation, book an appointment and upload your documents. For an income tax notice, request a callback and a CA will reach out.
