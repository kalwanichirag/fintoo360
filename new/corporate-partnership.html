FAQs

Questions HR teams ask us

Will HR or the employer see an employee’s financial information?

No. Employees interact directly with Fintoo for their personal financial journey, and their financial information stays private with Fintoo. We don’t take any employee financial data from HR.

Does Fintoo sell financial products?

No. Fintoo is a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser (INA000020031) offering zero-commission advisory. We don’t sell any product, so our guidance is not tied to what we earn from one.

How much work does this create for HR?

Very little. Fintoo AI is entirely self-serve: you enable access and employees use it on their own. We support the launch with announcement material, a townhall or financial wellness session, and email and WhatsApp communication.

Who is personalised financial advisory meant for?

CXOs, founders and senior leaders whose financial lives involve more complex decisions, such as ESOPs, portfolio restructuring, tax planning and retirement. Each gets dedicated advisory support and ongoing portfolio reviews.

Can we start with just one of the two programmes?

Yes. You can offer financial wellness to every employee, personalised advisory to your leadership, or both. We’ll discuss what fits your organisation in the 20-minute conversation.
