02

Nature of Activities

Fintoo Wealth provides investment advisory services to its clients in accordance with the terms of the applicable Investment Advisory Agreement and based on the client's financial circumstances, investment objectives, risk profile, investment horizon and other relevant information.

Any information, educational material, market commentary, financial planning content, calculators, tools, illustrations or other material made available through our website, mobile application, digital platforms or other communication channels is intended to provide general information and/or facilitate understanding of financial and investment concepts.

Such general information or educational content should not, by itself, be construed as personalised investment advice unless expressly provided as investment advice by Fintoo Wealth in accordance with the applicable regulatory framework.

03

Investment Risks and Disclaimer

Investments in securities and financial products are subject to market risks. There can be no assurance that any investment objective, financial goal or expected return will be achieved.

Past performance is not indicative of future results. Any historical returns, illustrations, projections, examples or performance-related information presented by Fintoo Wealth are provided for informational purposes and should not be construed as a guarantee or assurance of future returns.

Investment decisions should be made after considering the applicable risks, investment objectives, financial situation, investment horizon and risk tolerance of the investor. Investors are advised to carefully read all relevant offer documents, scheme documents, terms and conditions and other applicable documents before making any investment decision.

Registration granted by SEBI or any certification obtained by the Company or its personnel does not in any manner guarantee the performance of Fintoo Wealth or assure any returns to investors.

04

No Guarantee or Assurance of Returns

Fintoo Wealth does not provide any guarantee, assurance or promise of a fixed or favourable return on any investment or investment strategy.

Any reference to potential returns, historical performance, market trends, asset allocation or investment outcomes is for illustrative and informational purposes only and should not be construed as a promise or assurance of future performance.

05

Client Responsibility

Investment decisions are ultimately the responsibility of the client. Clients should independently consider their investment objectives, financial circumstances, risk tolerance, liquidity requirements, investment horizon and other relevant factors before making any investment decision.

The client is responsible for providing complete, accurate and updated information to Fintoo Wealth for the purpose of providing suitable investment advice. Any advice provided by Fintoo Wealth is based on the information and circumstances made available by the client and may need to be reviewed or revised if there is any material change in the client's circumstances.

06

Information and Illustrations

The information, data, illustrations, calculations, charts, market commentary and other material made available by Fintoo Wealth are compiled from sources believed to be reliable. However, Fintoo Wealth does not warrant that such information is complete, accurate, current or error-free at all times.

Any illustrations, projections or calculations are indicative in nature and are based on assumptions that may change over time. Actual investment outcomes may differ materially from any illustration or projection due to market conditions, taxation, charges, changes in interest rates, economic conditions, investor behaviour and other factors.

07

Conflicts of Interest

Fintoo Wealth maintains policies and procedures for identifying, managing and disclosing actual, potential or perceived conflicts of interest in accordance with applicable laws and regulations. Where a conflict of interest arises, Fintoo Wealth shall take appropriate steps to ensure that the interests of clients are appropriately protected and that applicable disclosures are made.

Fintoo Wealth and/or its associates, directors, officers or employees may, where permitted under applicable laws, have financial or other interests in securities, products, issuers or entities that may be referred to in its communications or advisory activities. Any material conflict of interest relevant to the investment advice being provided shall be appropriately disclosed to the client.

08

Disciplinary History

As of the date of this disclosure, Fintoo Wealth shall disclose its disciplinary history, if any, in accordance with the applicable regulatory requirements.

Disciplinary History: Fintoo Wealth has neither been suspended nor debarred from doing business by SEBI or any other authorities; nor has its certificate of registration been cancelled by SEBI at any point in time.

The Company shall make appropriate disclosures regarding any regulatory action, suspension, cancellation, debarment or other material disciplinary proceeding, as required under applicable law.

09

Research / Advisory Material

Any research, market commentary, educational material, financial planning content or other information disseminated by Fintoo Wealth is intended for informational and/or advisory purposes, as applicable.

Such material should not be construed as an offer, solicitation or recommendation to buy or sell any security except where the communication is expressly provided as investment advice in accordance with the applicable regulatory framework.

Investment advice, wherever provided, is based on the client's disclosed financial circumstances, investment objectives and risk profile and is subject to the terms of the applicable Investment Advisory Agreement.

10

Third-Party Information

Fintoo Wealth may, where appropriate, use information, data, market information, research or other material obtained from third-party sources. While reasonable care may be taken in selecting such sources, Fintoo Wealth does not guarantee the accuracy, completeness or timeliness of information obtained from third parties.

Clients should independently verify material information before taking any investment decision where appropriate.

11

Use of Artificial Intelligence and Technology

Fintoo Wealth may use technology-enabled tools, including artificial intelligence (“AI”), machine learning, generative AI or other technology solutions, for certain activities such as productivity, data processing, research support, analysis, communication, customer support, educational explanations or other operational purposes, as may be applicable from time to time.

Fintoo uses Artificial Intelligence (AI), machine learning models and automated analytical tools in certain features of its platform, including Mutual Fund Analysis, Stock Analysis and Retirement/Financial Independence Planning. These tools may generate Buy, Hold or Sell indications for mutual funds and stocks, and may provide an indicative retirement/financial independence age and suggested actions to work towards such goals. AI-generated outputs are based on available data, user-provided information, assumptions and applicable methodologies and are intended to assist users in their financial decision-making. Such outputs do not guarantee investment returns or achievement of financial goals.

Where AI or technology-enabled tools are used in connection with investment advisory services, Fintoo Wealth shall remain responsible for the investment advice provided to its clients and shall use such tools in accordance with applicable laws, regulations and internal policies.

AI-generated or technology-generated information may contain errors, omissions or inaccuracies. Such information should not be relied upon independently for making investment decisions.

Fintoo Wealth shall take appropriate measures in relation to the confidentiality and security of client information and shall comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements concerning client data and information.

  • Fintoo Wealth Private Limited and its associates do not have actual or beneficial ownership of 1% or more securities of the subject company(ies) as at the end of the month immediately preceding the date of the relevant advisory communication, except as may be specifically disclosed, if applicable.

The above disclosures shall be updated as and when required under applicable laws, regulations and regulatory directions.
