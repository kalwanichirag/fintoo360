FinCoins power AI calculation data points. These data points compare income, expenses, investments and inflation across financial scenarios to calculate your investment and retirement outlook.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move between Basic, Pro and Max whenever your planning needs change.

Is my data safe with Fintoo?

Yes. Fintoo uses bank-level security to keep your data safe, so you can focus on your financial future.

Which plan should I choose?

Start with Basic for essential analysis. Move to Pro for deeper calculations and advanced planning, or choose Max for the highest data-point allowance, an additional goal and priority support.
