How HRA exemption is calculated

Your HRA exemption under Section 10(13A) is the smallest of three amounts — not the full HRA you receive, and not the full rent you pay. Whichever of the three limbs is lowest is what you actually get to claim as exempt; the rest of your HRA is added back to your taxable salary.

This only applies under the old tax regime — the new regime doesn't allow HRA exemption at all, so if you've opted for the new regime, your entire HRA is taxable regardless of rent paid.
