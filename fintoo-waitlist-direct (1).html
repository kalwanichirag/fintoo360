Questions

Everything people ask before joining.

What is Fintoo AI?

Fintoo AI is an AI-led personal financial planning app for India. It securely connects your banks, brokers, mutual funds, insurance policies and loans into a single live view, reads how you actually behave with money, and tells you what to buy, sell, hold or change next.

When does the app launch?

1 September 2026, on Android and iOS. Everyone on the waitlist gets in first, along with double the starting Fincoins.

Is my financial data safe?

Fintoo AI connects through regulated account aggregators using read-only access. That means it can read balances and holdings to build your picture, but it cannot move, withdraw or transact your money.

Do you sell mutual funds or earn commission?

No. Fintoo AI advises rather than distributes, so no recommendation is shaped by what earns us more. It sits one level above execution platforms instead of competing with them.

What will it cost?

There is a genuinely useful free plan with mutual fund and stock analysis. Pro is ₹1,999 and Max is ₹4,999, adding retirement-age planning, debt restructuring guidance, expense analysis and tax optimisation insights.

Can I see what Fintoo AI would tell me, before it launches?

Yes. Our free Financial Clarity Score and report takes about two minutes. It scores how clearly you can see your money, then works out your earliest possible retirement age, what corpus you'd need, your cover gaps and the three things to fix first.

Who is Fintoo AI built for?

Working professionals and business owners across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Pune and Delhi NCR who already earn, save and invest — but have never been able to see whether all of it adds up to a plan.
