The Hidden Risk

Why Most Taxpayers Make
Costly Mistakes

Error Detected

Missing Foreign Asset Disclosure

Foreign accounts, properties, and investments left unreported — a trigger for income tax notices and heavy penalties under FEMA.

Error Detected

Wrong Capital Gains Calculation

Confusing short-term and long-term gains, incorrect cost basis, or missed indexation benefits leading to excess tax payment.

Error Detected

Incorrect Tax Regime Selection

Choosing the wrong regime without analysing your actual deductions — potentially paying lakhs more than necessary.

Error Detected

Missed Deductions

Leaving behind 80C, 80D, HRA, LTA, and NPS deductions that are legitimately yours — simply from not knowing they apply.

Error Detected

ESOP Reporting Errors

Incorrect perquisite valuation, timing of tax events, or failure to disclose unvested options — common for startup employees.

Error Detected

Incorrect Advance Tax Reporting

Missing advance tax deadlines or miscalculating liability results in interest levied under Section 234B and 234C.

"Your tax return is more than a form. It's a financial declaration."

One mistake can lead to notices, penalties, or excess tax payments that take years to resolve. Don't leave it to software.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How is this different from ClearTax or Quicko?
+
ClearTax and Quicko are software platforms where you fill in forms yourself. Fintoo gives you a real Chartered Accountant in a live video call who files alongside you, explains every entry, and ensures nothing is missed — especially for complex situations like ESOPs, foreign assets, or multiple income sources.
Will I speak to a real CA? Not a support agent?
+
Absolutely. You are directly connected with a practising Chartered Accountant, not a support executive or bot. Your CA is assigned based on your specific income profile and will handle your filing personally.
What documents do I need to share?
+
Typically: Form 16(s) from employer(s), bank statements, capital gains statements from brokers, foreign asset details if applicable, and rent receipts. Your CA will send you a customised checklist after the introductory call based on your situation.
Can you help with ESOP taxation?
+
Yes. We handle ESOP perquisite reporting at exercise, capital gains on sale, RSU vesting, and cross-border ESOP complications for employees of foreign-listed companies. This is one of our most common specialisations.
Can you help with foreign asset disclosure?
+
Yes, including Schedule FA reporting, foreign bank accounts, overseas property, PFIC holdings, and DTAA treaty benefits. Missing these disclosures can result in significant penalties — our experts handle this routinely.
Can NRIs use this service?
+
Yes. We serve NRIs across the US, UK, Singapore, UAE, and other countries. We handle residential status determination, DTAA claims, Indian source income, and repatriation planning.
How long does the full filing take?
+
The core session is 45 minutes. After the free 15-minute intro call and document collection, most returns are filed within 2–3 working days from the time of your session.
Is my financial data secure?
+
Yes. All document uploads use 256-bit encryption. We operate under strict data privacy standards and your information is never shared with third parties. Fintoo is a SEBI Registered Investment Advisor.
What happens after I book?
+
You'll receive a confirmation with a secure meeting link. Before the call, we'll send a short intake form and document checklist tailored to your income profile. Your CA will review this before your session.
Is the consultation online only?
+
Yes — fully online via video call. This allows you to access our experts from anywhere in India or abroad, without any travel or waiting room time.
