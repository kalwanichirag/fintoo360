How NPS works at retirement

Your contributions build a market-linked corpus until you retire. At exit, at least 40% of that corpus must go into an annuity — a plan that pays you a fixed monthly pension for life. You can withdraw the remaining amount as a lump sum, and up to 60% of the total corpus is tax-free.

The monthly pension itself depends on the annuity rate the insurer offers at the time — not the return your NPS corpus earned while accumulating. It's fully taxable as income in the year you receive it.

Monthly pension  =  Annuity corpus × Annuity rate / 12
