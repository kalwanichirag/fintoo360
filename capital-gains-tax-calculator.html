How capital gains are taxed

Whether a gain is short-term (STCG) or long-term (LTCG) depends on how long you held the asset — 12 months for listed equity/equity funds, 24 months for property, and 36 months for debt funds bought before April 2023. Equity gains are taxed at a flat special rate under Sections 111A/112A regardless of your income tax regime. Debt fund and property STCG, on the other hand, are added to your income and taxed at your slab rate — which is where the old vs new regime choice actually matters.

The July 2024 budget removed indexation for most assets and cut the LTCG rate to 12.5%. Property bought before 23 July 2024 keeps a grandfathering option: pay 20% with indexation, or 12.5% without — whichever is lower.
