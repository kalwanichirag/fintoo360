Old regime vs new regime

The old regime lets you claim deductions — 80C investments, HRA, home loan interest, and more — against a higher set of slab rates. The new regime offers lower slab rates and a bigger standard deduction, but almost no other deductions.

Which one costs less depends entirely on how much you can genuinely deduct. As a rule of thumb: the more of your income already goes into 80C-eligible investments, rent, or a home loan, the more likely the old regime works out cheaper.
