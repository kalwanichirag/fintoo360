What the score means

Four bands. Most Indian professionals land in the middle two.

Clarity isn't about how much you have. It's about how much of it you can actually see, and whether anyone is looking at the whole picture rather than one slice of it.

Planning assumptions

    This report is an educational estimate produced from the figures you entered, not investment advice or a regulated financial plan. It assumes your entire monthly surplus is invested and left untouched, that returns are steady rather than sequenced, that every rupee of savings is reachable in an emergency (in practice much of it will be locked or illiquid), and that you have no other income in retirement. Real markets deliver returns unevenly, and a bad sequence early in retirement matters more than the average. Investments are subject to market risk — read all scheme-related documents carefully.

    Questions

    About the score, and about Fintoo AI.

    What is a Financial Clarity Score?

    It measures how clearly you can see and control your own money across five areas: how many places hold it, whether you know your net worth, how recently you reviewed it, whether your goals are written down, and whether you know the age you could retire. It's scored out of 100.

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Your score and headline findings appear immediately, without entering a single detail. Your name, email and number are only needed if you want the full written report sent to you.

    Do I need to link my bank accounts?

    No. The Clarity Score asks about how you manage your money, not for access to it. Account linking happens later, inside the app, through regulated account aggregators with read-only access — Fintoo can never move or withdraw your money.

    What is Fintoo AI?

    India's first AI platform for personal finance. It connects every bank, broker, mutual fund, insurance policy and loan into one live view, reads how you actually behave with money, and tells you what to buy, sell, hold or change next. It launches 1 September 2026.

    What happens after I get my report?

    Nothing you didn't ask for. You'll get your report, then a message when early access opens on 1 September. No sales calls, and you can leave the list from any email we send.
