How this calculator works

Your monthly expenses are first inflated to what they'll cost at the age you retire. That future monthly expense is turned into a retirement corpus — a lump sum big enough to fund inflation-adjusted withdrawals every year from retirement until your life expectancy, assuming the leftover corpus keeps earning your post-retirement return.

Whatever your current investments will grow into by retirement is subtracted from that corpus. What's left is the gap a new SIP needs to close — shown either as one constant flat SIP, or as a smaller starting SIP that increases every year if you choose the step-up option.

Corpus = W × [1 – (1+r)⁻ⁿ] / r × (1+r)
W — expense in year 1 of retirementr — real return after inflationn — years in retirement
