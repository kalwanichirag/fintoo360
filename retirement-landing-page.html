Questions people usually ask

Questions people usually ask before planning their retirement.

How much money do I need to retire comfortably in India?

It depends on your lifestyle, retirement age, inflation, life expectancy, healthcare costs and existing assets. A calculator gives an estimate; a plan validates the path.

Is ₹1 crore enough for retirement?

For many families, ₹1 crore may not be enough after inflation and long retirement duration are considered.

How does inflation affect my retirement corpus?

Inflation raises future monthly expenses. The formula FV = PV × (1 + r)^n is used to project today’s expense into the retirement year.

Does the calculator include healthcare expenses?

The calculator gives an indicative lifestyle projection. A full plan should also include a separate healthcare and emergency reserve.

What if I already invest through mutual funds, NPS or EPF?

Those assets should be mapped into your retirement projection to understand the remaining gap and asset allocation required.

Can Fintoo help create a complete retirement plan?

Yes. The team can help review your number, investment gap, tax impact, risk cover and retirement income plan.
