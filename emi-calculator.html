How your EMI is calculated

Every EMI is the same fixed amount each month, but the split between principal and interest inside it isn't fixed — early instalments are mostly interest, since interest is charged on the outstanding balance and that balance is highest at the start. As you keep paying, more of each EMI chips away at the principal.

A prepayment doesn't reduce your EMI — it reduces the outstanding principal directly, which shortens how many months you end up paying interest for. Even a modest extra amount every month can cut years off a long home loan.

EMI  =  P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / [(1+r)ⁿ − 1]
P — loan amountr — monthly interest raten — tenure in months
