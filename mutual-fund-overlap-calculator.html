What is portfolio overlap?

If you hold two mutual funds that both own large positions in the same stocks, you're not as diversified as owning two funds suggests — you're effectively doubling up on the same bets. Overlap measures exactly how much of your money, by weight, sits in stocks common to both funds.

A high overlap doesn't automatically mean one fund is redundant — different fund managers, expense ratios, and mandates still matter — but it's a useful check before adding a second fund purely for "diversification".

Overlap %  =  Σ min(wA, wB) for every stock common to both funds
wA — stock's weight in Fund AwB — stock's weight in Fund B
